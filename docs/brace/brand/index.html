Ma marque

Raison d'être

  • Why: give everyone the full power of AI on their own machine, without the hassle: make using AI fluid, simple and human.
  • How: three gestures, in this order.
    1. It reads. Before asking anything, BRACE reads what the project already says: its tracking files, its decisions, its journal.
    2. It asks. What it cannot know, it asks, or marks "to fill in". It does not guess.
    3. It writes it down, you decide. What is decided, and why, is written in the project, in files you can read, fix or delete. BRACE tells you when it thinks you are wrong. You make the call.

One system, not several tools to find and assemble yourself.

  • What: a free, open-source plugin for the AI agent that works on your machine (Claude Code first, also works with Antigravity). It keeps the tracking files current, puts all your projects on one page, handles reminders and the calendar, and builds your brand through meetings and documents you validate one by one. Its skills share one folder. Only what is shipped is named here: the coming design work is not.

Positionnement

For people who run several projects with an AI on their machine and check what they get back, BRACE is the AI you are on the same page with: it reads what it can, asks what it cannot know, writes down what you decide. Unlike tools that remember for the AI or promise a result in minutes, it keeps everything readable, editable and yours.

Émotion

Serenity, resting on trust: you know it is handled, you no longer have to think about it. Trust comes first and brings the serenity; it shows in projects that are true to you: you recognise yourself in your work.
