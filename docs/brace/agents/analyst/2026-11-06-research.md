# Competitors, audience and existing brand: BRACE rebrand (2026-11-06)
Asked by: brand (Alice, account director), meeting 2 preparation. Depth: full.

Reused from 2026-10-19 (not redone): level 1/2/3 list and the sources in `2026-10-19-competitors.md` and `2026-10-19-audience.md`. Updated here: star counts, the product moves that matter for the rebrand, the new "AI branding tools and AI agencies" level, new audience voices (entrepreneurs, with the widened target), and the existing-brand audit. No name availability check (the name is kept).

## Summary
- The hypothesis holds: AI branding tools exist, and they are a different market from BRACE's. They sell a logo and a kit to non-designers (Looka, Brandmark, Tailor Brands, Canva), or a one-shot strategy document on the web (Servo, BrightMyBrand, BrandGap.AI). None I found keeps the brand as a readable record inside the project, used by the AI that builds it afterwards.
- The most important move is from Anthropic itself: Claude Design (research preview, 2026-04-17) learns a team's design system from its codebase, and the `marketing` plugin of its knowledge-work repo says it can "enforce brand voice". The "design is coming" part of BRACE will meet the host vendor's own product.
- "AI agency" products (MarkGrid, MarkBase AI) sell marketing execution with "agency killer" claims and big numbers: the opposite of BRACE's restraint. They are not brand work, and this tone is an open contrast.
- Level 2 packs keep growing (superpowers 313k stars, ECC 298k, claude-mem 114k, Spec Kit 148k, BMAD 55k on 2026-11-06). claude-mem now says "every agent", which narrows the "several hosts" angle.
- Audience: entrepreneurs and makers describe the same pain as developers, in business words ("consultant who gets amnesia every Monday"). Hand-kept context docs die from lack of upkeep. Misalignment and mess voices from non-developers are thin (gap, see Unverified).
- Existing brand: BRACE is almost invisible publicly (1 star, no description, no topic, no site, zero mentions found). The old name still shows in the README, the marketplace file, the bridge plugin and the repo's own status file.

## Added beyond the brief
- Anthropic's own brand and design moves (Claude Design, `marketing` plugin, `brand-guidelines` skill): the real threat to "design is coming".
- Brand strategy tools (BrandGap.AI, Servo and others) alongside logo tools: the brief said "branding tools"; the market splits in three (visual kit, strategy document, marketing execution).
- Prompt kits for ChatGPT/Claude as the "do it by hand" substitute for brand work.
- Namesakes of the old name on GitHub.

## Open spaces
- **Brand as a living record in the project.** Branding tools produce a PDF or a kit; BRACE's brand files live next to the code and the AI reads them when it writes.
- **Strategy and voice, not only the logo.** Level 4a barely mentions strategy or voice on its pages.
- **Calm instead of numbers.** The AI agency products shout figures and "replace the agency". The brief refuses number one-upmanship; this contrast is free to take.
- **A human-paced process.** Meetings, documents, validation, versus "instant logo" or "minutes". BrandGap.AI says it supports strategists; nobody else I read offers a staged process with the client.
- Still free from 2026-10-19: readable memory, project hygiene, multi-project portfolio with real follow-up, calm tone, curated toolbox.
- Narrowed: "shared across agents" (claude-mem now claims every agent).

## What it means
- The entrepreneur pain is the same as the developer pain, said as "onboarding", "consultant", "brand guidelines I paste again". The wider target does not need a different problem, only different words.
- "Doc that I forget to update" is the most repeated complaint. It supports "kept current by itself" over "memory".
- Trust: two voices fear silent wrongness. It supports readable, editable memory and "it asks when it does not know".
- "Mess" is mostly said by observers about others' AI-built work, not by the users themselves. I did not find a first-person non-developer saying "my AI leaves my project messy".
