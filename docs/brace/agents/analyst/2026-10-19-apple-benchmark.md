# Market (brand benchmark: Apple): BRACE (2026-10-19)
Asked by: brand skill (first brand session). Depth: quick.

## Summary
- Apple's official values are mostly societal: accessibility, education, environment, inclusion, privacy, supply chain. BRACE's draft values are behavioural: honesty, ask, disagree, stay light. They sit on different levels and don't compete.
- Real overlap: simplicity, few words, and "it just works". "It just works" is a widely quoted slogan that I did not confirm on a primary page.
- Gaps: BRACE has nothing on privacy or accessibility. Apple has nothing like "dare to disagree" or "never guess".
- The official Apple voice material I found is thin: an "informal voice" with contractions. The personality adjectives all come from commentators, so they are secondary.

## Comparison with BRACE's draft
- **Matches:**
  - Few words: Apple is concise with short sentences, and BRACE has "few words" and "stay light".
  - Calm and understated: Apple is "confident yet understated", and BRACE is discreet and calm.
  - Warm and plain: Apple's informal voice against BRACE's warm slider.
  - Works without friction: this is the owner's Mac/iPhone comparison. I did not verify the slogan itself.
- **Differs:**
  - Apple is aspirational and premium ("revolutionary", "empowering", per commentators). BRACE is rather serious with wit, reassuring and frank.
  - Apple's voice rarely disagrees with the user. BRACE dares to.
  - Apple's stated values are societal. BRACE's are behavioural.
- **Apple has, BRACE lacks:**
  - Privacy as a headline value. BRACE runs on the user's machine, which could support a similar claim, but check it against what BRACE actually does first.
  - Accessibility as an explicit value.
  - A published plain-writing standard (the Style Guide), which BRACE could mirror for its own strings.
- **BRACE has, Apple lacks:** total honesty, "never guess, ask", "dare to disagree", "propose, never impose", and token-lightness. These are BRACE's differentiators.

## Added beyond the brief
- **Apple's AI framing and the Style Guide:** BRACE is an assistant, not a device, so Apple's assistant positioning is the closer comparison.
- **The "values vs marketing" editorial:** added as a caution.

## Open spaces
- An assistant that states honesty and disagreement as values. Apple's voice stays smooth and agreeable.
- Lightness as a visible promise (few tokens, little noise). Apple sells simplicity, not frugality.
