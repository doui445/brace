## What each one teaches the route
- **Route 1.** Things proves calm can carry a whole brand when the design itself is the proof. Basecamp shows the "against the bloat" stance. Both sell one skill (tasks, projects); neither has to connect several competences, which is BRACE's challenge here.
- **Route 2.** No reference sells an "atelier" in software. Superhuman and Linear support the rhythm and care, not the agency framing, which fits the strategist's advice not to present BRACE as an agency.
- **Route 3.** Obsidian is the closest to BRACE's mechanism (readable files, yours). Signal shows how to claim trust: by a stated rule applied everywhere, with a nonprofit structure behind it. BRACE has no such structure yet, and the strategist already flags that privacy limits are undecided.

### 1. Superhuman: confirmed, keep it (with a limit)
