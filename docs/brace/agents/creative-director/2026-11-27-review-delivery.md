## What I checked
- The wordmark holds as one family. R, A, C, E share the B's even stroke, rounded ends and the same cap height. The designer's own A and C are credible: the rounded A apex echoes the brace ends, the C opens cleanly. No letter looks borrowed from a font.
- The brace still holds the word: same width as the name, tip up, same stroke weight as the letters.
- Large and small sizes: legible in the README mock (about 300 px wide), on the dark README and in the terminal. B petrol and RACE ink reads as intended. On dark, light petrol B and paper letters hold.
- Icon: in the before/after sheet, the B now sits centred in the square, the round and the rounded square, and stays crisp at 16/32/48. In the avatar sizes (16 to 96 px, light and dark) it reads centred.
- Deck: no em dash, no Bricolage-in-logo claim, the old test slide is gone, 13 slides and numbering consistent. Slide 5 (type) correctly says the logo is now drawn. Guidelines and board say the same.

## Send-backs
1. Brand: slide 2, the "why" says the B is "celui dont la taille reprend la pointe de l'accolade". It means nothing and is wrong (the stroke weight matches the brace, not a size, not the tip). Say "dans le trait du B de ton avatar, le même que celui de l'accolade", or cut the clause.
2. Brand: slide 4 promises "rond comme carré" but shows only rounded squares and no before/after. Either add the round crop (the designer's `check/icon-check.png` or the avatar sizes) or soften the claim. The centring is the client's complaint, so show it.
3. Brand (minor): slide 13 lede says "sur le nouveau dessin" but neither item mentions it; harmless, drop it if you want it tighter. The deck theme path still points to `files/delivery/sources/deck-theme.css`: only the CSS, fine, but note it is the old folder.
4. Designer (minor, not blocking): guidelines line 73 has a stray space before the period ("l'épaisseur .").

## Notes
- Taste, for the designer to look at, not a send-back: the B-to-R gap is a little wider than the others (R-A-C-E sit tighter), so the B reads slightly detached, partly because of its colour. Also the R's top-left corner shows a small notch worth a look at large size. Olivier should view A and C before the files are engraved, as the designer asks.
- Letters are strokes with a clipPath, not outlines. Fine for browsers and GitHub; a printer or Canva will want outlined versions. The board is "prête à envoyer à un imprimeur", so brand should tell the client an outlined set is a follow-up, or ask the designer to produce it.
- Lower limit stays at 96 px; legible at 72, so a cautious choice, acceptable.

## Verdict
Ready to show once brand fixes slide 2 (sentence) and slide 4 (show the round crop or soften). Both are quick, deck-only; no designer rework needed before the meeting.
