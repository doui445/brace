Route 1The honest ledger
The idea

Every hour,
accounted for.

Tally presents itself as the record a freelancer can show a client without a second thought: plain, exact, never padded. The brand speaks of trust rather than speed.

Who it is for

The freelancer billing by the hour who gets asked "what took so long?".

Against whom

Trackers that sell productivity scores: nobody sells the plain record.

The risk

"Honest" can sound defensive. The route holds through proof, not through the word.

Proposed tagline
The timesheet your client believes.
One-liner: Tally keeps an exact record of your hours, ready to send.
