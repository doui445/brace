# Brand platform

Read by `SKILL.md` once a route is chosen (`brand_stage: adjustments`).
The team writes the documents in the backstage that follows meeting 2
(`references/backstage.md`); meeting 3 presents them and the client
validates them, one at a time.

## The documents, in this order

1. **Brand platform** (`platform`, by the strategist): feeds
   `FOUNDATIONS.md` (content in `references/foundations.md`) and
   `STRATEGY.md` (content in `references/strategy.md`).
2. **Editorial guide** (`editorial-guide`, by the copywriter): feeds
   `VOICE.md` (content in `references/voice.md`).
3. **Manifesto** (`manifesto`, by the copywriter): feeds the brand story
   in `FOUNDATIONS.md`.
4. **Name**, only when one is needed (no name yet, a working name, a
   rebrand): `references/name.md`.

A personal brand adds the parts listed in `references/personal.md`.

## The meeting

1. Open it as a meeting: what the team wrote since last time, in two
   lines.
2. For each document, in order: show it rendered (its page in
   `documents/`, or a `doc` slide of the meeting's deck for the short
   ones), say in three lines what it settles, and ask: keep it, change a
   line, or say what is missing. A line the client cannot answer stays
   "to fill in".
3. A change: ask the agent who wrote it (a new brief with the change), or
   fix a single line yourself in its Markdown, then re-render. Never write
   the document yourself from scratch.
4. Once the client says yes, write the brand file it feeds (the project's
   memory that the skills read), with a link to the document.

## Resumed from 1.0

A project started on BRACE 1.0 (an old stage mapped here by the
`legacy:` line in `SKILL.md`) has no chosen route, no `BRIEF.md` and no
`SESSIONS.md`. Then:

- The brand files already written stand in for the chosen route. A
  document the client already validated is kept and never redrafted;
  propose a change only if they ask.
- Start at the first missing document, in the order above.
- Create `SESSIONS.md` with a first entry saying where the work resumed.
- Offer to pre-fill `BRIEF.md` from the existing files; if the client
  declines, leave it out. Never invent a route or a brief.

## Closing the meeting

Close it (`SKILL.md`): the minutes, the next appointment, then the team
prepares the delivery. Set `brand_stage: delivery`.
