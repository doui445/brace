# A mission started before 1.2

Read by `SKILL.md` when a mission's `- plan:` line has `platform` (any of
them), or when there is no `- plan:` line. Such a mission is finished
entirely as it was planned: its meetings, its documents (flat in
`documents/` and `presentations/`) and its stages. Never mix in a 1.2
meeting or document.

**Inside this folder, `references/<name>.md` means
`references/legacy-1.1/<name>.md` when that file exists here**; otherwise
the current file (foundations, strategy, voice, personal, revision, the
agents' briefs), which did not change in substance.

## The 1.1 mission

| Phase | Meeting | Reference | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `contact` | first contact and proposal | `legacy-1.1/contact.md` | `brief` |
| `framing` | framing workshop | `legacy-1.1/workshop.md` | `brief` |
| `strategy` | strategic recommendation | `legacy-1.1/presentation.md` | `presentation` |
| `platform` | brand platform (platform, editorial guide, manifesto) | `legacy-1.1/adjustments.md` | `adjustments` |
| `delivery` | the handover | `legacy-1.1/delivery.md` | `delivery` |

- `contact`, `framing` and `delivery` are always there; `strategy` and
  `platform` depend on the scope. No look (logo, colours, type) in a 1.1
  mission: if the client wants it, finish this mission, then propose a
  mission for the look (`references/contact.md`, a brand delivered by 1.1).
- No `- plan:` line (a mission started before plans existed): the full
  plan above.
- The backstages: `legacy-1.1/backstage.md`; the documents:
  `legacy-1.1/documents.md`; a name: `legacy-1.1/name.md`; a rebrand:
  `legacy-1.1/rebrand.md`.
- The agents' methods changed in 1.2. Brief each agent of a 1.1 mission
  with its 1.1 method, `legacy-1.1/agents/<agent>.md`, given in full in
  its brief: "this is a 1.1 mission, follow this method, not your own".
