# Delivery

Read by `SKILL.md` at the last session (`brand_stage: delivery`).

## The delivery page

A final presentation, `presentations/<YYYY-MM-DD>-delivery.html`, that
works as the brand's portal: the brand in five lines (purpose,
positioning, emotion, tagline, one-liner), then one slide per document
(`slide doc`), then what comes next. Copy the template if needed
(`references/presentation.md`).

## The test with real people

A **preference and comprehension test**, the form the trade's testing
tools use:

- Show the two or three finalists (taglines, positioning statements,
  names) to **5 to 10 people** from the target.
- Three questions:
  1. "Which one makes you most want to know more, and why?"
  2. "What does it promise, in your view?"
  3. "Explain to me in one sentence what this product does."
- BRACE prepares the page or the form (a short HTML page in
  `presentations/`, or the text of a form to paste into a survey tool).
  The user shares it (people close to them, their target, or a paid panel
  if they want one). BRACE reads the answers the user brings back.
- Write the results in `docs/brace/brand/DECISIONS.md`. Never simulate a
  result, never present a guess as a test.
- The user may skip it: write "not tested yet" in `DECISIONS.md` and move
  on.

Offer the interview guide again (`references/workshop.md`) if the user has
not run it.
