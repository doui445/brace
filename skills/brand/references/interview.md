# Interview and foundations

Read by `SKILL.md` at the interview step, then for the foundations.

## The interview (`brand_stage: interview`)

1. Ask what the project is, who it is for, and which competitors the user
   already knows. One question at a time; reuse their words.
2. Ask the depth of the research (quick or full, recommend full for a first
   brand session), then launch the analyst in the background with two
   subjects: **competitors** (with their messaging and tone, not only their
   features) and **audience** (what people actually say, in their exact
   words). Brief format: `SKILL.md`, `## The analyst`.
3. Give the user an **interview guide** to run with 3 to 5 real people from
   the target, whenever they can:

   > Ask for real past stories, not predictions: "Tell me about the last
   > time you…". Ask what they were trying to get done when they reached
   > for a product like this (the job they hired it for). Write down their
   > exact words.

   What the user brings back later feeds the personas
   (`references/strategy.md`). Never invent answers in its place.

For a personal brand, also run the entourage test from
`references/personal.md`.

## The foundations (`FOUNDATIONS.md`)

While the analyst works, in this order, one part at a time. Show each part,
get a yes, then write it.

1. **Purpose**: why, then how, then what. The why is a mission, never "we
   make X" ("speed up the transition", not "make cars").
2. **Personality**: describe the brand as a person. Place it on adjective
   sliders and keep the user's own adjectives:
   - discreet ↔ expressive
   - serious ↔ light
   - warm ↔ neutral
   - reassuring ↔ stimulating
   - reduce ↔ express

   Then values, vision, and an archetype if one fits naturally.
3. **Core emotion**: one emotion the brand should leave, not a list. It is
   checked against every later choice.
4. **Brand story**: the customer is the hero, the brand is the guide. Name
   the enemy (often abstract), the hero's starting state (what they feel
   before), and the transformation.

For a personal brand, add the journey and the type
(`references/personal.md`).

A part the user cannot answer yet stays marked "to fill in".

When the foundations are written, set `brand_stage: strategy`.
