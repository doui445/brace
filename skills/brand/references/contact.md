# First contact

Read by `SKILL.md` at the start of a mission (meeting 0, `brand_stage:
brief`, no `BRIEF.md` yet). The first contact sets the relationship: the
client meets the agency, hears how we will work together, and accepts a
proposal.

## Before saying anything

Read the project (`README.md` or its front page, the notes, `STATUS.md`)
so that the first words are about it, not about the agency.

## The first message

In the working language (the language the client writes in), as the
account director, with her first name from the catalogue:

1. **The client and the project first.** A greeting, and one or two
   sentences that show you read it: what it does, who it seems to be for.
2. **A word on the agency.** What it does for a client like them (the
   positioning, the words, the documents a brand stands on), and how we
   work: a framing workshop, the team works between meetings, we meet again
   to present, the client decides, we deliver.
3. **The team**, one line each, first names and roles from the catalogue,
   as she would introduce colleagues ("<first name>, our research analyst, will
   look at who else speaks to your clients").
4. **The proposal**, introduced as the next thing to look at together.

Short: the client reads it in under a minute. Never a feature list, never
"I am a tool that...".

## The proposal

A client document (`references/documents.md`), written by the account
director, rendered and shown:

- **The scope**: what we build together (the brand's substance: purpose,
  positioning, voice, words; not the visual identity yet) and what the
  client receives (the documents, one by one, in their client space).
- **The meetings**: the five, in one line each, and what each one asks of
  the client.
- **The time**: about an hour of the client's own time over the mission;
  the team's work between meetings takes about ten minutes each time.
- **The budget**: measured on a small project, about 15 million tokens
  read, almost all from the cache, and about 120 thousand written; a bigger
  project costs more.
- **What the client can do at any time**: stop, come back, ask, change
  their mind; everything is kept.

Ask: "Shall we go with this proposal?" (yes, a change, not now). Yes:
write the first `SESSIONS.md` entry (the proposal accepted, with a link to
the document), then offer to hold the workshop now or at an appointment
(`SKILL.md`, the closing of a meeting). The workshop starts in the same
session when the client wants: `references/workshop.md`.
