# Personal brand

Read by `SKILL.md` when `brand_profile: personal`. Adds to the interview,
the foundations and the strategy.

## Interview and foundations

- **Entourage test**: ask a few people who know the person "describe me in
  five words", then cross the answers with how the person sees themselves.
- **Journey**: why this person, the path that leads here. In
  `FOUNDATIONS.md`.
- **Type**: professional (freelance, consultant), influence (the name is
  the business) or leadership (an executive). In `FOUNDATIONS.md`.

## Name

Real name, pseudonym, business brand, or hybrid (build the person first,
then a business brand). A descriptive pseudonym is a trap the day the
person pivots.

## Strategy additions

- **Packaging**: a short bio (a clear direction at a glance and some
  curiosity, no sales link first); a description of two lines at most,
  then the main link and one simple call to action; a handle with no
  underscores or emoji; a pinned post that guides newcomers; one hub video
  or demo first.
- **Indicator**: a figure and a date ("10 clients by June"), never the
  number of followers.
- **Channels and cadence**: one channel at the start; regularity before
  anything else; one role per format. An owned medium (a site, a
  newsletter) comes last.

## Keep in mind

One personal brand at a time, and a long-term investment.
