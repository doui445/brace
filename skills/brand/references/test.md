# Test

Read by `SKILL.md` at the test step (`brand_stage: test`).

Before the brand is presented, show the tagline, the positioning statement
and the name (if it is new) to real people from the target, ideally the
ones who decide.

- **Five-second test**: show it for five seconds, then ask what they
  remember and what it promises.
- **Between two or three finalists**: ranked preference, recall, fit with
  the brand.
- **Targeted questions**: "What does this promise? Who is it for?". Never
  "Do you like it?".

Write the results in `docs/brace/brand/DECISIONS.md`.

The user may skip this step: write "not tested yet" in
`docs/brace/brand/DECISIONS.md` and move on. Never simulate results, and
never present a guess as a test.

Then go to the presentation (`SKILL.md`, step 7).
